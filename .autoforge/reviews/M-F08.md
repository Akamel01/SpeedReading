## 4. Heap estimate method

`heapEstimateMB = (charBytes + (memAfter - memBefore)) / 1024 / 1024` with `charBytes = Σ chunk.text.length * 2` (UTF-16). **Sound as a screen, not exact**: it double-counts chunk text already inside the heap delta and ignores GC noise/object overhead, but it over-counts rather than under-counts and lands 2.7× under the 100MB budget with stable values across runs — adequate for a pass/fail budget check.
